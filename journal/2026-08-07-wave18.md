## The structural finding

The sharpest part is not the score but the diagnosis. Two pins
(`guard_uses`, `guard_errors`) exist specifically to prevent a past error
from recurring — and they **score MISS on all eleven commits where that error
was live**:

> "their locators were fitted to the sentence as repaired. They would have
> reddened a run with a message that was false. Structural rather than a slip:
> **every pin is written after the repair it pins, so the class of errors a pin
> is blind to is the class it was created in response to.**"

That is a general law about retrospective safeguards, discovered by replay
rather than argued: a guard built from a fixed bug is fitted to the *fixed*
text, and therefore cannot see the *broken* text it was meant to catch. It
generalizes every instance of this pattern the observatory has logged —
julia2's `|| true` (a guard that could never fire), instance 4's test suite
(a guard protecting the defect), and now the pins (guards that fire but never
decide).
