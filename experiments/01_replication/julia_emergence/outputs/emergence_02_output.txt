=== II. inside the race: chance, or history? ===
  400 raced releases, outcome vs first effective mover:
    first mover = mark 1  →  outcome ▮·   ×197
    first mover = mark 2  →  outcome ·▮   ×203
  outcome fully determined by first mover? true

  Read it three ways, and watch the perspectives split:
  CLASSICAL:  no chance at all. The schedule is a hidden
    variable; given who moved first, the outcome is fixed.
    Determinism is saved by enlarging the state.
  QUANTUM-FLAVORED: from INSIDE the latch there is no
    schedule — marks 1 and 2 see a symmetric state resolve
    to one of two memories, irreducibly. The 'measurement'
    outcome has no cause expressible in the latch's own
    vocabulary. (Analogy only — no Hilbert space was built.)
  RELATIONAL:  'which memory' is not a fact until the race
    completes — the fact exists relative to the completed
    interaction, and BEFORE it, the question has no referent.
  CONVERGENCE: all three agree on the statistics.
  DIVERGENCE:  they disagree on where the fact LIVES.
  STILL LACKING, all three held together: none says why
  there is a schedule AT ALL. Time is still handed to the
  world from outside — by us. The debt is now twice flagged.

=== III. history becomes a thing ===
  2000 identical latches after one forbidden race: ·▮×1013, ▮·×987
  Two populations, no law distinguishing them — only their
  schedules did. A 'thing' (_01) was a loop that persists;
  after the audit: a thing is a loop that persists AND
  carries the scar of the race that selected it. Identity
  = attractor + history of arrival. Neither term optional.
